## First WhatsApp reply

Thank you for writing. This is a preliminary international-patient inquiry, not a diagnosis.

Please send:
1. What you want to change
2. Any previous surgery or filler
3. Front and profile photos in even light
4. The city you would fly from and a flexible date range

A coordinator will outline the next step. An in-person assessment in Shanghai is required before any treatment plan is final.
